# Instrument team

Pages for the SOLAR-C EUVST instrument team. They are left out of the menu and the search, and search engines are asked not to index them, but anyone with a link can read them, so please keep the link within the team.

- [Science cases](science-cases.md): the Concept Study Report's science cases as ready-made ECLIPSE configurations, to test changes to the instrument or to ECLIPSE against.
- [Pinhole stray light](pinholes.md): visible light and unattenuated EUV let through by defects in the aluminium filter.
- [Reading out without a shutter](readout.md): what the SW camera records if the CCDs stay illuminated while a frame is cleared and read, and how to simulate it.
- [Pixels and the PSF](pixels-and-psf.md): how a synthesis is laid onto the detector's pixels, where the PSF comes in, and the telescope's blur across the slit.
